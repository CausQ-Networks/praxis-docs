Praxis is an open-source platform for secure access to your apps, infrastructure, and AI. Connect and protect your workforce, AI agents, and infrastructure, wherever they are.

Praxis gives people access to exactly what their role allows (web apps, SSH, remote desktops, private hosts, and AI models) without exposing anything to the public internet or opening up a whole network with a legacy VPN. Share a link and someone can sign in from a browser, or install a client to reach private resources by name. Every access decision follows identity and gets logged.

## What You Get

- **Access in minutes, with just a link.** Clientless, browser-based access to web apps, terminals, and remote desktops. No jump boxes, no open ports.
- **A faster, safer VPN replacement.** Users reach only the resources they are granted, over direct connections, signing in with their existing work account.
- **A smaller blast radius.** A compromised login cannot roam your network. Sync users and roles from your identity provider and offboard someone once, everywhere.
- **Audits with less work.** Every access decision is logged with who, what, and when.
- **AI under the same rules.** An identity-aware AI Gateway puts roles, budgets, and session history in front of cloud and self-hosted model APIs, so AI agents follow the same policy as people.

## Why Praxis

Connectivity and security belong together. SASE platforms like Cloudflare One, Zscaler, and Prisma Access got that idea right, but delivered it as heavyweight, closed, cloud-locked stacks. Praxis brings them into one platform with a single identity and policy model, and keeps it simple enough to run yourself.

- **Open source and auditable.** Every component, from the server to the clients, is on GitHub. See exactly how traffic is handled and how access decisions are made.
- **Lightweight by design.** The control plane runs on a modest server. A user-space connector sits in your private networks.
- **Run it your way.** [Self-host](/self-host/quick-install) or use [Praxis Cloud](https://app.praxis.causq.com/auth/signup). No lock-in.

## Core Concepts

Praxis is organized around a few pieces you will use everywhere:

1. A **server** is the control plane. It holds identity, policy, and coordination. You can use [Praxis Cloud](/about/praxis-cloud-vs-self-hosted) or [self-host](/self-host/quick-install) the same software.
2. **[Sites](/manage/sites/understanding-sites)** connect the networks where your applications and infrastructure live.
3. **[Resources](/manage/resources/understanding-resources)** are the specific things users are allowed to reach: web apps, SSH, desktops, private hosts, and AI providers.
4. **[Clients](/manage/clients/understanding-clients)** give devices a private path to those resources.
