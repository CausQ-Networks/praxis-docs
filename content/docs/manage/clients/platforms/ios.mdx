## Install

- [Praxis on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/praxis-client/id6757407406) - This is the official page to download the latest Praxis app for iOS and iPadOS.

### Installation Steps

1. **Download and install the Praxis app**

   Download and install the Praxis app from the App Store using the link above.

2. **Launch Praxis**

   Open the Praxis app from your home screen.

3. **Install the VPN configuration**

   When prompted, allow Praxis to add VPN configurations to your device.
   
   You may be asked to enter your device passcode or use Face ID/Touch ID to authorize the VPN configuration.

4. **Log in with your Praxis account**

   Log in on your Praxis Cloud account or your self-hosted Praxis instance.

5. **Connect to Praxis**

   Tap the Connect button to establish a VPN connection.

## Configure

<Note>
This section covers the general settings for this platform. DNS, MTU, and other connection settings are the same on every platform and are on [Platforms](/manage/clients/platforms#connection-settings).
</Note>

### Dynamic Island and Live Activity

When enabled, Praxis shows connection status in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.

### Connect Automatically On

Choose when Praxis may connect automatically. Tap **Connect** to enable on-demand for that interface; **Disconnect** disables it.

**Cellular** connects automatically while the device is on cellular.

**Wi-Fi** connects automatically while the device is on Wi-Fi. You can limit which networks that applies to:

- **Any Wi-Fi Network** connects on every Wi-Fi network.
- **Only these Wi-Fi Networks** connects only on the networks you list.
- **Except these Wi-Fi Networks** connects on every network except the ones you list.

## Update

Updates are delivered through the App Store.

## Uninstall

1. On the Home Screen, touch and hold the Praxis app icon.
2. Tap **Remove App**, then **Delete App**.

Deleting the app removes the Praxis VPN configuration from the device. Reinstall from the App Store if you want to use Praxis again.
